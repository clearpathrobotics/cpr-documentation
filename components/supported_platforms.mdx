### Proton Requirement {#proton-requirement .no-number}

Middleware support other than Fast DDS requires `clearpath_firmware` version `3.0.0` or later and the Proton MCU protocol. Please ensure your robot's firmware is updated and the MCU protocol is configured correctly. See the [MCU Protocol configuration documentation](config/yaml/platform/mcu) for setup instructions.

:::
