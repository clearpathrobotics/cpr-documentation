### Husky Variants _(AMP Autonomous Mobile Platform, and Observer)_

Husky A300 can be configured as a Husky AMP or a Husky Observer.

The Husky AMP adds sensors to the robot, and our [OutdoorNav](/docs_outdoornav_user_manual/) software.
The sensors and software create a robot with autonomous navigation, and able to be driven through camera teleoperation.
Refer to the [Husky AMP](../../../solutions/husky_a300_amp) user manual for more details.

The Husky Observer includes all the equipment from Husky AMP, and then adds Autonomous Wireless Charging, and a Pan-Tilt-Zoom Camera.
Refer to the [Husky Observer](../../../solutions/husky_a300_observer) user manual for more details.
